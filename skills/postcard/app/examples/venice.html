Day by day

Sat 16 OctArrivalCulture
Arrive, Rialto and San Marco.
  • Drop the car, then water taxi or bus into the city.
  • Rialto Bridge and Piazza San Marco in the evening, once day trippers thin out.
Sun 17 OctCultureScenic
Doge's Palace and the islands.
  • St Mark's Basilica: walk up entry ended in 2025, so book a timed slot online. BOOK AHEAD
  • Doge's Palace, no closing day. MUST-SEE
  • Murano and Burano by vaporetto in the afternoon.
Mon 18 OctCultureScenic
Dorsoduro, slower.
  • Accademia Gallery.
  • Quieter canals south of the Grand Canal, the area most day trippers skip.
Tue 19 OctMarketTravel
Rialto fish market early, then the train.
  • Pescheria fish market, Tue to Sat 7:30 to noon only. The one open morning of the stay. MUST-SEE
  • About 2 hours by train to Florence.

Know before you go

  • City access fee hits day trippers on peak dates.
  • Overnight guests are exempt but need a QR code from their stay.
  • No 2027 fee dates announced yet.

Festivals & Events

  • LIKELYBiennale Musica, about Oct 10 to 24. A yearly discipline, unlike Art and Architecture.
  • LIKELYVenice Design Week, about Oct 9 to 18.
  • UNCERTAINVenice Fashion Week, starts about Oct 19, just as the stay ends.
