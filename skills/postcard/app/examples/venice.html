VENICE · MURANO · BURANO · OCT 2027

Three nights in Venice, slowly, and away from the queues.

Rialto and San Marco in the evening, once the day trippers have left. The Doge’s Palace and the islands on Sunday, the quiet canals of Dorsoduro on Monday, and the Pescheria fish market on the one morning it is open. St Mark’s Basilica now needs a timed slot booked online, so do that first.

Music, Theatre, and Dance are annual Biennale disciplines (unlike Art/Architecture, which alternate years) — so an October Biennale Musica edition is a safe bet most years.

Fun fact

Venice sits on roughly 118 small islands linked by close to 400 bridges. The whole city has been slowly settling into the lagoon for centuries, while the sea around it has risen about 32cm since 1872 — which is exactly why the MOSE flood-barrier system now closes off the lagoon during high tides.
